Spatial Video Rendering

Test page for projected video (360°, 180°, wide-FOV) rendered in WebKit's media controls.

Before you start

  1. Open Develop › Feature Flags (Safari Technology Preview).
  2. Enable Spatial Video Rendering. It is off by default.
  3. Reload this page.

How to tell it is working

  • Working: dragging on the video rotates your view inside the scene.
  • Not working: the video plays flat and dragging does nothing — the flag is off, or the build lacks the feature.

This page deliberately cannot auto-detect the feature: the renderer draws into the media controls' user-agent shadow root, which page scripts cannot inspect, and the projection metadata is intentionally not exposed to the open web. Dragging is the check.
