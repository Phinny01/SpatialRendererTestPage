Spatial Video Rendering

Test page for projected video (360°, 180°, wide-FOV) rendered in WebKit's media controls.

Before you start

  1. Open Develop › Feature Flags (Safari Technology Preview).
  2. Enable Spatial Video Rendering. It is off by default.
  3. Reload this page.

Declared projection

A 360° clip that carries no projection metadata at all, so x-webkit-projection is the only thing that can make it render as a sphere. Leave the value on auto and it plays flat.

Camera

Where the camera looks, and how wide. These set x-webkit-fieldofview, x-webkit-yaw and x-webkit-pitch through their reflected IDL attributes, so moving a slider should move the view immediately.

Directly on the video: drag to look around and scroll to zoom (a smaller field of view is more zoomed in). Those move the camera without changing the attributes; the sliders follow along via webkitcameramoved, and the readout shows all three — the IDL attribute, the content attribute, and the live camera.
